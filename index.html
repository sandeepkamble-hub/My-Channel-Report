Bottom line

People find this channel through search and stay for the longer short films. Vyatha and Sorry Beta hold viewers for two to five minutes, while Shorts barely get past 14 seconds. With nothing new uploaded, there has been no reason for anyone to subscribe.

  1. Publish a new long-form film. No uploads this month is the biggest reason growth has stalled.
  2. Lean on what holds attention. Add end screens and a pinned comment on Vyatha and Sorry Beta pointing to your other films and the subscribe button.
  3. Sharpen search titles and thumbnails on the older films, since search brings 77% of views but viewers watch only about 11% of a video on average.
